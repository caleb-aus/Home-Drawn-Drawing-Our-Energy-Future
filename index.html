About our project

Home Drawn: Drawing our Energy Future is a collaborative art project focusing on energy justice in the Illawarra Renewable Energy Zone (UREZ). The project aims to bring awareness to renewable energies and its advantages through the often unheard voices of children in the Illawarra; who may be living in low socioeconomic households/areas and may not be able to reap such benefits with ease. By engaging directly with the community, we hope that our project could bring emphasis to the unfair distribution of renewable energy, and gather a larger attention on it in hopes of enacting a positive change for the future.

We believe that community collaboration and in this case, working alongside children, is important because of how their perspectives can often reveal experiences that may be overlooked. This also allows the project to move beyond speaking about children, and instead create opportunities for children to speak themselves through their creative responses, becoming a collective voice that adults, researchers and decision-makers can encounter and discuss, influencing energy outcomes such as the UREZ.

Created by Caleb K, Qi Z, George M, Gwenwyn BB, Sam, and Tsulandi RG
